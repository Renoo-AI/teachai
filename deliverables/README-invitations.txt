TEACH AI — INVITATION LAB 02

Open vip-invitations.html in Chrome or Edge. Fonts and QR generation work offline.
Ten distinct layouts: Spark, Quiet, Ticket, Poster, Notebook, Gallery, Orbit, Ribbon, Blocks, Letter.
Arabic-first with embedded Cairo, Noto Arabic and Amiri. English/French copy presets are available.
Click a card, change its composition, text and colours. Type and QR size controls apply to every layout.
Enable Drag elements to move individual items. Alternatively choose an element and adjust its X/Y offsets in millimetres.
Apply style to all changes colours/type while preserving each card's composition.
Save editable HTML or export project JSON to preserve your edits.
Print A4 portrait, 100% / actual size, background graphics ON, headers/footers OFF. Each invitation is 100 x 50 mm.
The ten default cards contain SAMPLE codes, not activated VIP coupons. Replace with your active codes before sharing.
QR opens your access page; teachers enter the printed code. The URL is not printed but is visible when scanned.

Cookies and storage

One cookie, only after you enter an access code. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The cookie

NamePurposeLifetime
ta_accessRemembers on this device that you entered a valid access code, so the app stays open to you. It holds a signed token with the code’s identifier and an expiry date, not your name or email. First-party, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure on https.Up to 30 days, or until the code expires or is withdrawn

This cookie is strictly necessary to provide the invite-only service you asked to use, so it is set without a banner. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Browser storage (not cookies)

KeyWhat it keeps
teachai.uiYour language (French, Arabic or English)
teachai.theme, teachai.accentLight or dark mode and your accent colour
teachai.aiYour AI settings, including a DeepSeek token or NVIDIA key if you chose to enter one. It stays in your browser and is sent only with your own requests.

Nothing here is sent to advertisers or analytics services, and we do not load third-party scripts or fonts.

Controlling them

You can delete the cookie and the stored preferences at any time in your browser’s settings. If you delete the cookie you will be asked for your access code again, and a one-time code may need to be reissued by us: write to us through the contact page.

More about the data we handle is in the Privacy Policy.
